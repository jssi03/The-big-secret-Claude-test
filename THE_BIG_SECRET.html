Simple rules

  1. Everyone gets a secret role. Never show your phone.
  2. Read your mission and your ability.
  3. Talk, bluff, lie, make alliances and ask questions.
  4. Listen for the host's clues and plot twists.
  5. When voting opens, vote for who you think is the Mastermind.
  6. Most votes wins the blame. If it is the Mastermind, investigators win. If not, the culprit team wins. Ties favor the culprits.
  7. Keep it fun. No personal attacks and no searching bags or pockets.
